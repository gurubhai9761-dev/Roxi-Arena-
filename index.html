Rules

Play clean or sit out

Breaking a rule means disqualification. Updates and room times are posted in the WhatsApp group after you register.

Not allowed

  • ✕
    No PC playersMobile players only.
  • ✕
    No hacksAny cheat or modified client means disqualification.
  • ✕
    No bypassNo bypass tools or workarounds of any kind.
  • ✕
    No team-upsSolo and Duo players cannot work with other players or teams.
  • ✕
    Misha character is bannedYou cannot pick the Misha character in any mode.
  • ✕
    Duo: no same active skillThe two partners must use different active skills.

Fair play and highlights

  • ●
    Recording is mandatory for reportsIf you see a suspicious team or player, send the recording of that match. A report without a recording is not accepted.
  • ●
    Highlights within 48 hoursEach tournament's highlights are uploaded to the YouTube channel @29rxp within 48 hours.
  • ●
    Video link in your groupThe highlight video link is sent to your WhatsApp group once it is uploaded.
  • ●
    More updates after you registerRoom time, room details and changes are posted in the WhatsApp group.
